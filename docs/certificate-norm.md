# Certificate norm arithmetic

Continuous surface/curve certificates first screen exact homogeneous coefficient
norms with verified outward intervals. Conclusive bounds avoid large rational
squares and their reductions. Tight boundaries, unavailable finite intervals,
and zero or subnormal limits use the original exact rational calculation.
Accepted upper bounds retain exact square-root checks. Tolerances and proof
work/depth limits remain unchanged.

Independent exact squared inequalities cover signed gauges, large denominators,
threshold neighbors and extreme floating-point ranges. A headless benchmark
compares complete trimmed surface edits against a frozen geometry revision;
both variants must produce identical B-rep geometry and topology. Native support
fitting is outside the measured interval. These debug-build measurements cover
the retained cases and machine; they do not establish general or Rhino
performance parity.
